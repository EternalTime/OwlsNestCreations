## The app

Explore the Brownian motion of a particle due to the tiny atoms around it, investigate the kinetic phase transition in active matter flocking, or play with pendulums strung together in chaotic harmony.
This app collects some of the most important toy models that physics students will encounter through their undergraduate and graduate work, and turns them into tunable simulations that help build intuition.
Each model also includes a mathematical description of it, as well as citations to the literature, so that students have a place to start in their research.
Build intuition, research, and you've taken the first steps to becoming a physicist!

The collection will grow with time.
Keep it updated to have the latest applets in your collection.

The app is free, doesn't collect any data from you, and is here to help students in their educational adventures!
