The sheets are real elements rather than drawings, because they are glass: what they blur is the ground behind them, which is being pinched by the two bodies on the canvas. Nothing here bends that ground. Each sheet is a row of the application's list: a spacetime's short name and its tags, on clear glass. The names and the tags are the collection's own, from `metrics_index.json`. {%- endcomment -%}
